Smart Clogging Detection

System monitors differential flow rates across filters. If >70% clogging is detected, it triggers an automated failover to the redundant branch.

02

Autonomous pH Balancing

Integrated dosing logic injects base or acid from secondary containers to neutralize main line water to a perfect 7.0 pH.

03

Operational Continuity

Redundant solenoid valves ensure zero-downtime during filter maintenance, keeping critical systems online 24/7.
